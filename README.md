# OCHO Card Game

### Match. Play. Win.

**OCHO** is a fast-paced color-matching card game for **2–6 players**, combining simple rules with strategic choices, powerful action cards, and unexpected reversals.

Match the top card by **color, number, or symbol** and race to empty your hand before your opponents. Use Skip, Turn, Draw 2, Wild, and Color Crush cards to disrupt their plans—but be ready, because every penalty carries the possibility of an OCHO Escape.

## What Makes OCHO Different?

The number eight is more than the game's namesake. It can become your greatest defense.

When you are forced to draw cards because of a **Draw 2** or **Color Crush**, draw them one at a time. If you uncover an **8**, the penalty ends immediately and you play that card regardless of the active color. The color of the 8 takes over, potentially changing the entire round.

This special **OCHO Escape** mechanic keeps every draw suspenseful and gives players a chance to turn a devastating penalty into a game-changing comeback.

## Powerful Action Cards

Build your strategy around cards that can instantly alter the flow of play:

- **Skip** — Make the next player lose their turn.
- **Turn** — Reverse the direction of play.
- **Draw 2** — Force the next player to draw up to two cards.
- **Wild** — Choose the new active color.
- **Color Crush** — Choose the active color and force the next player to draw up to four cards.
- **OCHO Escape** — Draw an 8 during a Draw 2 or Color Crush penalty to stop drawing and immediately play it.

## Don’t Forget to Call OCHO

When you play a card and have exactly one card remaining, call out:

> **“OCHO!”**

Forget to announce it, and another player may catch you and force you to draw two penalty cards.
